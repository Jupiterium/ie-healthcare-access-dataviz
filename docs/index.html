Waiting or paying? Is it getting harder to get healthcare in Ireland?

In Ireland, waiting has become a bigger reason than cost for people going without medical care, and the hospital waiting list has grown over the same years.

Waiting: Ireland (2.1%) is above the EU average (1.2%), but far below Finland (7.3%) and Estonia (6.9%). Cost: Ireland (0.6%) is below the EU average (1.0%), and Greece (8.8%) is far ahead of everyone.

These are patterns, not causes. Values are small, so a difference of 0.1 or 0.2 points can be survey noise. Eurostat flags a break in Ireland's series in 2019 and 2020, so those years are not fully comparable. The survey covers all medical care, and the EU average is Eurostat's own weighted figure.
